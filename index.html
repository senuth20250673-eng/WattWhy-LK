Privacy Policy

1. Introduction: WattWhy LK values your household's privacy. Energy telemetry collected during the pilot is stored locally on the hub and encrypted in transit.

2. Data collection: We collect non-invasive telemetry only (optical pulse counts, ambient temperature, humidity and vibration signals). We do not record audio or video.

3. Use of telemetry: Data is used only to evaluate appliance breakdown, tariff forecasting and anomaly detection.

4. Third parties: We do not sell, rent or trade your usage data to third parties or advertisers.

Terms of Service

1. Pilot participation: Participation is voluntary and subject to hardware availability in selected provinces.

2. Safety compliance: Users agree never to open, modify or tamper with utility meter seals or electrical distribution boards. Any panel work must be done by a certified electrician.

3. Tariff calculations: Estimates are based on prevailing Sri Lankan domestic tariffs (CEB & LECO) and are informational forecasts only.
